Liam's dissertation, "Integrative Approaches for Cancer Characterization and Treatment: Combining Hyperspectral Imaging Techniques with Nonlinear Laser Ablation for Precision Oncology," ties optics to oncology. It covers compound-prism design, ultrafast laser ablation and nonlinear multiplexed excitation. Congratulations, Dr. Price!
