---
title: "Scialog: Advancing BioImaging team award"
date: 2021-08-10
---

In the first year of Scialog: Advancing BioImaging, ten multidisciplinary teams shared $1.15M in funding. The three-year initiative is supported by the Research Corporation for Science Advancement, the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative and the Frederick Gardner Cottrell Foundation. Bryan Spring and Barbara Smith (Arizona State University) received CZI funding for "Microendoscopy-Guided Diagnosis and Treatment of Early-Stage Ovarian Cancer."
