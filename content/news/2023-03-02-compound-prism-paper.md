title: "New software for designing compound prism arrays"
date: 2023-03-02
link: "https://doi.org/10.1364/OE.475175"
---

Liam Price, Julia Tatz and colleagues published a paper in *Optics Express* describing software for designing compound prism arrays. Compound prisms spread light by color with high efficiency and nearly linear dispersion over a wide band, which makes them valuable for spectroscopy and multiplexed imaging. They are also hard to optimize in commercial optical-design packages. The new tool is flexible, easy to use and much faster.

It is free for academic use on [GitHub](https://github.com/jdtatz/compound-prism-designer). For commercial licensing, contact Prof. Spring or Northeastern's [Center for Research Innovation](https://www.northeastern.edu/cri/).
