---
title: "Ryan Lang defends his dissertation"
date: 2021-08-05
---

Ryan defended "Multiplexed In Vivo Imaging and Analysis Methods for Optical Imaging Biopsy of Cancer." Arriving with a strong background in theoretical physics, he used it to tackle hard problems in imaging tumor heterogeneity and immune scoring with microendoscopy. Along the way he became a team leader across physics, microscopy and cancer biology, with eight papers published, under review or in preparation. Congratulations, Dr. Lang!
