Sudip's thesis, "Enabling More Precise, Effective, and Broadly Applicable Photoimmunotherapy through Systematic Target Identification and Protocol Refinement," identified new cancer biomarkers for photoimmunotherapy. It also studied how cells respond to photosensitizers and produced a flexible light-delivery device for photodynamic therapy. Congratulations, Dr. Timilsina!
