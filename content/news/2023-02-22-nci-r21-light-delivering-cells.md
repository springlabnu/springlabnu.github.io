---
title: "NCI R21 grant for light-delivering immune cells"
date: 2023-02-22
---

With the [Schaffer–Nishimura Lab](https://snlab.bme.cornell.edu/index.php) at Cornell University, the lab won a high-risk, high-reward NCI R21 award (CA263452, about $0.5M). The project aims to deliver light anywhere in the body for precision photomedicine, using tumor-homing immune cells that carry their own light source.
