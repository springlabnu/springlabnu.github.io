Rebecca Harman and colleagues published a spectral denoising method in *Biomedical Optics Express*. An n-dimensional Chebyshev or Fourier transform groups pixels by how similar their spectra are, regardless of brightness or position. The method then filters noise in that spectral space, so spatial detail is preserved. It reduces errors in separating fluorescent signals by up to 70% without blurring the image.
