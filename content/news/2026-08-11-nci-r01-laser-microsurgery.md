---
title: "New NCI R01 grant for precision laser microsurgery"
date: 2026-08-11
---

The National Cancer Institute has funded the lab's project "Precision cancer microsurgery using laser ablation-cooling with pulse bursts" (R01 CA296584, about $3M). Partners on the grant are Frank Wise (Cornell University), Ömer Ilday (Ruhr University Bochum) and Pablo Valdés (University of Texas Medical Branch).
