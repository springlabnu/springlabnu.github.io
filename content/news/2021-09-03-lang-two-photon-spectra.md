Ryan Lang measured two-photon excitation spectra of common fluorophores from 700 to 1300 nm. He found that exciting at their long-wavelength peaks lets multiplexed imaging reach deeper and with less photobleaching. The work is published in *Biomedical Optics Express*. Congratulations, Ryan!
