---
title: "Physical Sciences–Oncology Network grant for fractionated photoimmunotherapy"
date: 2023-06-23
---

The lab and its partners received an NCI Physical Sciences–Oncology Network award (U01 CA280849, about $2.7M) for "Fractionated photoimmunotherapy to harness low-dose immunostimulation in ovarian cancer." Collaborators are the Enderling lab (MD Anderson Cancer Center), Bob Gatenby and Rob Wenham (Moffitt Cancer Center), and Jose Conejo-Garcia (Duke).
