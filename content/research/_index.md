title: "Research"
description: "How the Spring Lab uses light to see and treat cancer: targeted photomedicine, multiphoton microendoscopy, fiber lasers and real-time image analysis."
---

Some cancer cells survive chemotherapy and hide in places surgeons and scanners can't see. We work on catching them with light. We design drugs that switch on only when light hits them inside tumor cells, and we build tiny microscopes that can watch those cells inside the body. Our work combines three areas: growing and testing tumor models, building lasers and microscopes, and writing the software and math that make sense of the images.
