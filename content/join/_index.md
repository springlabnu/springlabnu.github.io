We are always glad to offer research opportunities to students and to start new collaborations. Whether you are an undergraduate curious about research, a graduate student choosing a lab, or a postdoc with an idea, we'd like to hear from you.

## Undergraduate volunteers

Undergraduate volunteers work alongside graduate students and postdocs on real projects, and many have gone on to co-ops, awards, co-authored papers, and graduate or medical school (see [People](/people/) and [News](/news/)). Students from physics, bioengineering, biochemistry, engineering, data science and health sciences have all found a place here.

### Learning opportunities

As a volunteer you can gain hands-on research experience in:

- **Cell culture**: growing and treating cancer cell models
- **Optics**: lasers, microscopes and light-delivery systems
- **Electronics**: building and controlling lab hardware
- **Computational biophysics**: image analysis, simulation and modeling

The lab's specialized instruments and open-source tools are described on the [Platforms](/platforms/) page.
