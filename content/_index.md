After surgery and chemotherapy, a few cancer cells often survive, too small to see and resistant to drugs, and they can bring the cancer back. We build lasers, miniature microscopes and light-activated drugs to find those cells and destroy them precisely. We also use light to rally the immune system against the cancer. Our goal is cancer treatment that lasts longer and is easier on patients.
