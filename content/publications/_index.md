The papers below report what the lab has learned about imaging and treating cancer with light: new lasers and microendoscopes, light-activated drugs that target only tumor cells, and models of how tumors respond to treatment. Most entries have a free PDF copy. For citation counts and the newest preprints, see [Bryan Spring's Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=s_D5aXEAAAAJ&hl=en).
